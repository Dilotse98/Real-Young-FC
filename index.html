Why We Exist

Football is the tool.
Character is the goal.

In Sebokeng, Emfuleni, and the greater Vaal Triangle, young people face challenges that extend far beyond the football field — limited opportunities, unemployment, substance abuse, crime, and a lack of positive recreational outlets continue to affect countless talented individuals.

Real Young Football Club was established in 2024 on a simple, powerful belief: every young person deserves the opportunity to pursue their potential. Football teaches discipline, responsibility, teamwork, leadership, perseverance, and self-belief — the same qualities that build careers and communities.

Our mission extends beyond winning matches and climbing league tables. We're building an environment where young footballers develop as athletes, students, leaders, and responsible citizens — with the guidance, mentorship, and opportunity to build successful futures both on and off the field.

Investing in young people today creates stronger families, safer communities, and a brighter future for tomorrow.

Real Young Football Club
Vision

To develop exceptional footballers and responsible citizens, build a sustainable football institution rooted in community values, and compete among the highest levels of South African football — creating opportunities for generations to come.

Mission

To provide young footballers with quality coaching, mentorship, and competitive opportunity within a positive environment that promotes sporting excellence, personal growth, discipline, and community development.
